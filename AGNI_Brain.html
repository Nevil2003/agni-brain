The science

10 peer-reviewed papers across three pillars, plus the physics that sets every spec.
Physics that fixes the specs
  • Bowel sounds live at 100–500 Hz dominant, rarely >1000 Hz
  • Loudness ~60 dB SPL — quiet, easily buried
  • Event duration 10–4000 ms
  • Resting rate ~5–34 events/min

This single spec set drives sampling rate, filter band, mic choice and gating.

Comparable devices (proof it's real)
  • AbStats / AGIS — FDA-cleared for ileus monitoring (prospective ileus sensitivity ~63%)
  • Marshall IBS belt — ~90% sensitivity in proof-of-concept
  • SonicGuard (Fraunhofer) — long-term abdominal recording
Three strategic gaps = the moat
  • No unified hardware + Ayurvedic-scoring consumer product exists
  • No India-specific gut phenotype bowel-sound dataset
  • Consumer-scale patch form factor is unvalidated

04

Signal pipeline

From skin vibration to four labelled classes — all on-device.
CAPTURE
I2S @ 16 kHz

Mic in diaphragm, pressed & sealed to upper-right abdomen.

GATE
IMU motion gate

>200 mg pauses pipeline; 3s stillness resumes.

FILTER
Butterworth BP

4th-order, 80–1000 Hz band.

DETECT
Short-time energy

Adaptive threshold, 10–4000 ms duration gating.

CLASSIFY
1D-CNN (Edge Impulse)

MFCC → 4-class. Target ≥80–90% val accuracy.

record → laptop
→ Audacity (eyeball SNR)

Skip Zephyr RTOS until you need low-power production firmware. Prove signal first; never start with on-device classification. SB↔CRS must separate reliably before anything else.

05

The AGNI Score

Computed every 24h, relative to your own baseline.
AGNI = 0.40 · (Bowel-sound rate vs your baseline)
     + 0.25 · (1 − Pattern abnormality)
     + 0.15 · (Postprandial response)
     + 0.10 · (Temperature deviation)
     + 0.10 · (User symptom score)
// → 100 × Σ wᵢ · clip(normalise(featureᵢ)), Σ wᵢ = 1.00
Design intent

Sound carries the most weight; the body's slower signals refine it. Weights are a transparent product decision, not a fitted result — adjustable as validation data arrives.

Normalisation

normalise() is a per-user rolling z-score mapped to [0,1]. clip() bounds motion/noise outliers. Every sub-score runs against your personal, not a population, baseline.

Evolution note

The earlier formula carried a sweat-conductivity (EDA) term at 0.10. With the AD5941 dropped from v1, that weight moved to the user symptom score. Postprandial response replaced the older thermal-only refinement.

07

Clinical decisions

Riya's review → four resolutions, locked in Rev 1.1.
01 · Battery & BLE, honestly

Edge-compute only. One JSON packet per day over BLE — no raw audio leaves the patch. Honest PoC battery is ~3 days duty-cycled on 110 mAh, not 7.

02 · Motion gating, step by step

LSM6DS3 acceleration gate: >200 mg pauses the acoustic pipeline; a 3-second stillness window resumes it. Clothing-rub & speech share the 100–500 Hz band — gating is non-negotiable.

03 · Personal baselines

Days 1–3 silent calibration on first wear. All sub-scores run relative to the personal baseline, with a rolling 7-day recalibration.

04 · Score → action map

Every WhatsApp delivery carries a direct behaviour instruction for the day's state (Sama / Vishama / Tikshna / Manda) — a reading is never delivered without a next step. See §06.

08

Risks & honest notes

What kills this if you get it wrong.
1

Acoustic coupling is the bottleneck. A bare MEMS mic on skin barely registers ~60 dB bowel sounds against body + ambient noise. The diaphragm coupling + sealed gasket + firm adhesion is where most solo builders fail — expect most debug time here.

2

Real-world prediction is moderate, not magical. Even AGIS dropped to ~63% prospective ileus sensitivity. Set expectations: this is a wellness trend tool, not a diagnostic oracle.

3

The Agni mapping is an unvalidated hypothesis. Treat it as falsifiable (§06), with a fallback that survives a negative result.

4

Don't build the hardware company. The winning founders here are software-first. The moat is the Indian food model, the practitioner network and WhatsApp distribution — not the patch design. Source the device from an ODM (Pune / Hyderabad) at scale.

Regulatory

Stay in the wellness lane.
Positioning

Wellness / experimental device. Does not diagnose, treat or cure. Keep all claims to trend & lifestyle — never "diagnosis."

CDSCO

The wellness-vs-medical-device line under CDSCO is the boundary to respect. Crossing into diagnostic claims pulls you into medical-device regulation.

DPDP Act 2023

Gut/health data is sensitive personal data. Edge-compute (no raw audio leaving the patch) is both a privacy feature and a compliance asset.

12

Build plan

Four gated steps — pass before you proceed.
Step 1 · Capture & validate

INMP441 → XIAO over I2S, stream to laptop, save WAV. Couple diaphragm to upper-right abdomen, lie supine.

PASS: you can see & hear distinct bowel bursts above noise floor (SNR visibly >2–3×) in Audacity after an 80–1000 Hz bandpass. Fail → fix coupling before anything else.

Step 2 · Detect & classify

Python: bandpass → short-time energy → adaptive threshold → 10–4000 ms gating → events/min + rule-based SB/MB/CRS/HS. Then Edge Impulse: MFCC (13) + 1D-CNN.

PASS: events/min in sane range (5–34 resting); classifier separates SB vs CRS reliably; ≥80–90% val accuracy.

Step 3 · Temp + score

Add MAX30205 on I2C. Compute the five sub-scores; output AGNI 0–100 over BLE/serial.

PASS: end-to-end score updates live and moves sensibly fasting vs after a meal.

Step 4 · Wear test

Mount XIAO + diaphragm + MAX30205 + LiPo in a small enclosure; Tegaderm to skin. Record ~2h fasting + 40–90min postprandial; log symptoms.

PASS: stable adhesion, usable SNR seated, AGNI trend reproducible across 2–3 days on yourself.

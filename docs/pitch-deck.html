The Science

Not new science — proven
science, productized.

Primary evidence
AbStats / AGIS — FDA 510(k) K150782
Class II Electronic Stethoscope. MEMS mic + Tegaderm. Counts events/min.
Spiegel 2014 — J Gastrointest Surg
100% sensitivity, 97% specificity. N=49 patients. Postoperative ileus detection.
Craine 2002 — Gastroenterology
Sound-to-sound interval (SSI) at 640ms cutoff: 91% sensitivity, 100% specificity for IBS.
Cohen 2021 — Gastroenterol Rep
Distinguishes 360 kcal vs 1,110 kcal meals within 90 min. Red/yellow/green zones.
Technical evidence
Baronetto 2024 — JMIR AI
EffUNet on 136h, 27 subjects. 16kHz, 8-mic wearable. Public GitHub code.
Yu 2024 — PLOS ONE
Branchformer + self-supervised pre-training. Excels with limited labeled data.
Du 2018 — J Acoust Soc Am
Mathematical model of bowel sound generation. Spring-mass-damping physics.
Nowak 2021 — MDPI Sensors
50+ year review: automated BS analysis maturity is being translated to practice.

Regulatory & Risk

Positioning

Wellness / experimental device. Does not diagnose, treat or cure. Same lane as SUNA, consumer fitness trackers.

FDA predicate (K150782) proves the same approach is safe enough for clinical use. Our consumer version carries less clinical responsibility.

Key Risks

● Acoustic coupling — MEMS mic to skin is the hardest engineering problem. Most solo builder time goes here.

● Real-world prediction is moderate — trend tool, not diagnostic oracle. Even AbStats drops to ~63% prospective sensitivity in some studies.

● Agni mapping is a hypothesis — if it fails, the fallback is a validated wellness score. Product survives either way.

● DPDP Act 2023 — edge-compute (no raw audio leaves device) is a compliance feature.

Build Plan

Four gated steps.
Pass before you proceed.

Step 1 · Capture (Week 1-2)

INMP441 → XIAO over I2S. Record right lower abdomen, supine.

PASS: Distinct bowel bursts visible in Audacity after 80-1000 Hz BP. SNR >2-3×.

Step 2 · Count (Week 3-4)

Python DSP: bandpass → ST energy → adaptive threshold → events/min + SSI.

PASS: Rate in 5-34/min. SSI detects meal response.

Step 3 · Baseline (Week 5)

3-day personal calibration. Fasting AM + 90 min post-meal × 3 days.

PASS: Reproducible rate/SSI pattern. Rate increases post-meal.

Step 4 · Ship (Week 6-8)

BLE output → phone → WhatsApp daily trend. 10-person pilot.

PASS: Stable adhesion, usable SNR seated, trend reproducible across days.

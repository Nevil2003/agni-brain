[3] Craine 2002 — SSI for IBS Diagnosis Gastroenterology

BL Craine, et al. • PMID: 12076930
What it proves: Sound-to-sound interval (SSI) at 640ms cutoff gives 91% sensitivity, 100% specificity for IBS. Your strongest single biomarker.
"IBS patients: SSI = 452±35ms. Healthy controls: SSI = 1,931±365ms. Cut-off 640ms — 91% sens, 100% spec. p<0.0001."

TIER 1

[4] Du 2018 — Mathematical Model J Acoust Soc Am

Xuhao Du, Gary Allwood, K Mary Webberley, et al. • DOI: 10.1121/1.5080528 • PMID: 30557135
What it proves: Spring-mass-damping model of bowel sound generation. 4 sound types (SB/MB/CRS/HS) with durations, spectral centroids, and flatness values. Your physical foundation.
"Bowel wall = mass-spring-damper system. 4 parameters: IWC (wall dims), PI (pressure), CQ (contractions), CIT (timing). SB 18-58ms, MB 100-1030ms, CRS 119-1637ms, HS 73-763ms."
